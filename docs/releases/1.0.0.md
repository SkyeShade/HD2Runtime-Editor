**HD2R Editor 1.0.0**: an in-game editor for every value [HD2Runtime](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime) exposes. Press **F8** in game, or the key you bound on the game's keybind page.

Made for **[HD2Runtime 0.40.0](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime/releases/tag/v0.40.0)**. On an older HD2Runtime the pages and fields it lacks stay absent.

### New in 1.0.0

**Enemies, spawning and destruction**
- **Enemies:** Terminids, Automatons, Illuminate, Structures and Other Entities, with every enemy and structure HD2Runtime knows (1,178 targets). They show their recovered names, faction icons and colours, and full variant lists. Each covers size, shields, jammers, spawners, damage zones and typed attacks. Gameplay controls are listed first.
- **SPAWNING & DESTRUCTION:**
  - **Enemy Overview**, with per-front spawn sliders;
  - **Mission Difficulty**, with budgets and cooldowns;
  - **Spawn Rosters**, with weights by difficulty and the game's difficulty icons;
  - **Spawn Compositions** (capped at 32 enemies; the editor says when a recipe is capped);
  - **Demolition Templates** and **Destruction Models**.

  Spawn edits are applied aboard the ship and affect the next mission.
- **Pick your bots/bugs/squids:** a shortcut to its own picker, and pending spawn values that never fight with it.

**Missions**
- **Mission Overview** per front (Terminids, Automatons, Illuminate):
  - **one slider per original duration** (40 / 20 / 15 / 12 min), up to **41:40**, the game's own limit;
  - **FREEZE TIMER / RESUME TIMER**: the host freezes the running mission timer, and other players with HD2Runtime follow;
  - a **"Freeze automatically"** box per duration: when you host a mission of a ticked type, its timer freezes right after landing.
- Shared timers are marked. Ticking or changing one changes every mission and front that shares it.

**Helldiver and equipment**
- **Armor perks:** their own page, with 31 perks' numbers. The two equipped passives are now on the Helldiver page.
- **GameGuard warning:** the Helldiver's own stats carry a red GAMEGUARD RISK mark and note, because players reported GameGuard closing the game after tuning them. The armor class tables already had it.
- **Reload, heat, melee and throwables:**
  - reload on every reloadable firearm, and underbarrel reloads in **Underbarrel Weapon**;
  - Double-Edge Sickle heat, Variable RPM, Warrant modes and PLAS-39 charge;
  - **Melee** on each weapon page: hit volumes, repeat hits, damage and stamina;
  - throwables: launch and spin, four statuses and arming delay.
- **Projectile swaps:** the GL-28 belt-fed swap, the W.A.S.P. missile hit projectile and the G-109 Urchin pulse payload.
- **Supply Pack:** box capacity, start and refill, with the refill fields explained.
- **Weapon and grenade silhouettes** from the game's own HUD atlas, and **mod icons** in the MODS list.

**Easier editing**
- **RESET ITEM** next to EXPAND / COLLAPSE ALL. It resets every change you made to the item you're looking at, applied ones immediately and pending ones dropped. It asks first and names the items that share those values. Values another mod set stay.
- **Shared values are marked immediately** on every page that shares them. Before, it took several seconds.
- **The game's keybind page:** bind **Open / close HD2R Editor** (and HD2Runtime's settings) on Controls > MODS (Mod Bindings Menu). While that key is set, Settings shows F8 struck out with "MODS keybind". Empty it there and F8 works again.
- **Lighter when settled:** fields that are already applied stop being re-checked every frame and wake on an edit. Closed-window upkeep pauses. The F10 Performance graph names the editor's UI and work separately.
- **Translations:** every new text is in all 13 languages.

### Install
Install `HD2REditor-1.0.0.zip` with your mod manager (Echelon or HD2 Arsenal), next to HD2Runtime 0.40.0. It replaces earlier versions.

Tested offline against the HD2Runtime 0.40.0 source. **Live-tested:** the mission timer freeze (solo), the mission overview sliders and auto-freeze, RESET ITEM, and the MODS keybind display. Most new enemy, spawning and weapon fields are not live-tested yet.

### Known
GalacticMenuHotkey's Galactic Map shortcut inherits left click and A/Cross, so on the war table every click re-opens the map. This isn't the editor; rebind or disable that shortcut.
