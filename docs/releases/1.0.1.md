**HD2R Editor 1.0.1**: an in-game editor for every value [HD2Runtime](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime) exposes. Press **F8** in game, or the key you bound on the game's keybind page.

Made for **[HD2Runtime 0.40.1](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime/releases/tag/v0.40.1)**. It still runs on HD2Runtime 0.40.0; there the armor class rows stay editable with their GameGuard warning.

### New in 1.0.1

**Resets wait for Apply**
- **RESET** in Changes marks a field for reset: the row shows **PENDING RESET** and its button becomes **UNDO**. **Apply** resets it; **UNDO** or **Discard** keep the value.
- The same for **REVERT ALL** and **RESET ITEM** (both still ask first), the failed-value **RESET** in Browse and Errors, and **Del** in Browse. A pending REVERT ALL also untunes custom stratagems on Apply.
- Click a **PENDING RESET** tag in Changes or Browse to take the mark back.
- Marks are not saved in presets, exports or the crash checkpoint.

**Optimized application follows HD2Runtime 0.40.1**
- HD2Runtime 0.40.1 keeps the 0.40.0 application optimizations **off by default** (the 0.31 behaviour), because players reported the game failing to start with 0.40.0.
- The editor no longer turns them on at every start. Its Settings switch now saves the choice through HD2Runtime: it is the same switch as **F10 > Settings > Faster application (0.40.0)**.
- With HD2Runtime 0.40.0 the switch works as in 1.0.0.

**Armor classes and the damage curve are read only**
- HD2Runtime 0.40.1 no longer writes game.dll's own data, after a report of GameGuard closing the game following armor class edits. **Armor classes** and **Armor damage curve** show *read only (game.dll data)*, and their rows can't be edited.
- Kit piece weights and armor perks stay editable.

### Known
- The 1.0.1 builds were reported working with HD2Runtime 0.40.1 on 2026-10-19. The read-only armor rows were not part of that report; they are tested offline.
