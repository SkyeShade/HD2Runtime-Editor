**HD2R Editor 0.9.3**: an in-game editor for every value [HD2Runtime](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime) exposes. Press **F8** in game.

Made for **[HD2Runtime 0.31.2](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime/releases/tag/v0.31.2)**. It still runs on HD2Runtime 0.30.0 and later with fewer features: everything marked *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2)* below needs 0.31.2, and simply stays absent on an older Runtime.

### New in 0.9.3

**Your language**
- **Every text can be translated:** object names, subtitles, sections, groups, field labels, values (On / Off / None / Unlimited, statuses, fire modes) and messages. Settings > WRITE TEMPLATE now writes the complete template: the interface first, then every name and field your HD2Runtime shows.
- **Safe with any script:** Chinese, Japanese, Korean and other non-Latin text is never cut in the middle of a character, and Backspace removes whole characters. A translation that breaks a `%d` or `%s` shows the English text instead.
- **The game's language by default:** until you pick a language in Settings, the editor uses the translation file for the game's language, checked again each time the window opens (HD2Runtime 0.31.1 and later).
- **The game's own names** *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2)*: weapons, support weapons, throwables, backpacks, vehicles, boosters and stratagems are shown by the game's own name, in the game's language. Settings: **Use the game's names** (on by default). Search still finds everything by its English name, and presets and exports are unchanged.

**Pages that are easier to read**
- **Categories:** an object's fields are sorted into Projectile, Damage, Handling / Fire, Ammunition, Heat / Charge, Beam / Arc, Armory and Hellpod / Call-in.
- **Everything collapses:** every category and part opens and closes with a click, Enter or the arrow keys, and stays closed on every page until you open it again. **COLLAPSE ALL / EXPAND ALL** in the page header (Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down).
- **The direct hit is under Projectile,** with the round's flight and explosions, round by round (the AC-8's Flak gets its own set). Damage keeps only damage no round carries (beams, arcs, sprays).
- **Tidier layout:** a part with a single field no longer gets a header of its own, fields are indented under their part, and the DEFAULT, VALUE and SOURCE columns stay aligned down the page.

**Status effects**
- **Status slots 1 to 4** *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2; one slot before)*: every empty slot of a damage row, in order, so the GL-15 Evictor's blast can take both Gas and Gas Confusion. Slot 2 stays greyed out (IN ORDER) until slot 1 holds a status, the editor applies them in that order, and an exported mod writes them together. Acid Storm is offered wherever HD2Runtime 0.31.2 allows it.
- **Each slot's stats under the slot:** Status 1, Status 1 strength, Status 1 duration, Status 2 ... A duration is the status's own and shared by every attack that uses it, so it says "all uses". The separate "Primary status 43" sections (TX-41 Sterilizer and others) are gone.

**Weapons and projectiles**
- **What a swapped weapon fires:** while a projectile swap is set, the weapon's own projectile rows are set aside (HD2Runtime refuses them) and the page shows **Fires: <donor>**. With HD2Runtime 0.31.2 the donor's flight, direct hit and explosions are shown read-only, with a link to the owner weapon's page.
- **LAS-99 Quasar and RS-422 Railgun projectile swaps** *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2)*. The M-1000 Maxigun, GL-28 and other weapons HD2Runtime keeps read-only now show why.
- **LAS-99 Quasar: Cooling rate while overheated** *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2)*: this sets the lockout after an overheat (100 / this value, so 6.66 = 15 s). Cooling rate does not change it.
- **Payload explosion settings under the slot:** when an Impact or Expiry payload names an explosion, that explosion's damage, radius, armor penetration, statuses and so on appear right under the slot. These fields are shared with every weapon that sets that explosion off.
- **Support weapon payloads** *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2)*: Impact and Expiry payload on support weapon rounds, for each fire mode and ammunition (APW-1, AC-8 Flak, the Epoch's charge levels, the SG-88's rounds ...). The Spear, Commando and W.A.S.P. missiles take an impact payload only.
- **A new payload picker** *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2)*: all 139 explosions a payload can take, grouped (Weapons, Support weapons, Throwables, Orbitals, Eagles, Hellbombs, Sentries, Mines, Vehicles, Backpacks, Enemies, Other) and searchable. Each shows its damage, radius and warning badges (VERY LARGE, LINGERING, FIRE, SHRAPNEL, ARC, NO DAMAGE), and the highlighted one's warnings are explained. This includes the **EXCESSIVELY LARGE NUKE** (the Shredder Silo's blast). Its **300 MB LOAD** badge warns that two mission packages load first: up to 90 s and a hitch.
- **LOADING:** a value waiting for its game data to load shows LOADING on its row and in Changes. If the data cannot load, you get an error with the reason, and the value is not written.
- **Clearer labels:** a projectile's own damage reads "Direct hit", so the RL-77's 350 is no longer mistaken for its explosion. The RL-77's bomblets *(named Bomblets with HD2Runtime 0.31.2)* and the EAT-700's napalm shrapnel show on their weapon's page.

**Stratagems, vehicles and backpacks**
- **Hellbomb detonation:** the B-100 Portable Hellbomb shows its detonation, and the NUX-223 Hellbomb gets an entry of its own. Both Hellbombs and some mission objectives share this damage row, and the page says so.
- **Mission category:** Resupply is now called Mission (Resupply and the NUX-223 Hellbomb). Presets and keys are unchanged.
- **Vehicle mounts by name:** the TD-110 Maelstrom's Main cannon, driver gun and missile pods, and the TD-220 Bastion's cannon and Coaxial MG. The Bastion's coaxial MG notes that it is a single belt with at most 2047 rounds, the game's network limit (the editor keeps capacities to 2047 and magazine counts to 31).
- **Damage zones by their game names** on vehicle, backpack and stratagem pages ("Cockpit rear", "Radar", "Back hatch" ...), where HD2Runtime knows them.
- **Empty hellpod slots** are shown as Empty and can be filled (89 were missing before, including the EAT-411's second slot).
- **TX-41 Sterilizer hellpod** *(HD2Runtime 0.31.2)*: its items and slots are editable. Locked hellpod rows give HD2Runtime's reason.

**Presets**
- **IMPORT PRESET...** under NEW PRESET: the exact folder the editor reads, CHOOSE A FILE... (Windows dialog), IMPORT SELECTED, REFRESH and OPEN THE FOLDER. Large presets (up to 16 MB) are read a little each frame, with their progress shown, even while the window is closed. Every import ends in a message, a line on the import page and a line in the log: the fields imported, or why not (for example a JSON error with its line and column). ModBuilder project files are converted too.
- **The saved data limit:** HD2Runtime keeps at most 1 MB of saved data per mod. A preset that would not fit is refused with its size, so it is never silently lost at the next start.
- **Preset icons:** ICON gives a preset any stratagem or booster icon. If you build the editor yourself, you can also use your own pictures (tools/preset_icons.py). The icon travels in shared files, and older editors still read those files.
- **Tidy lists:** SHARED PRESET FILES and MODBUILDER PROJECTS open and close on their header.

**Errors at a glance**
- **Red markers:** a refused value marks its field (ERROR, value framed red), its object, its closed groups and its category.
- **ERRORS badge (or Ctrl+E):** lists every failed field with the value asked for, HD2Runtime's code and reason, and where the value came from (your edit, a preset, the saved session, a mod's value). Enter or a click opens the field. Ins writes the list to the log.

### Fixed in 0.9.3
- **Payload parts open at once** (confirmed in game): after picking an explosive payload, the explosion part that appears under the slot can be opened, closed and edited straight away. Before, you had to leave the weapon's page and come back.
- **Preset import:** importing a large preset worked but showed nothing, so it looked broken. Every import now says what happened (see IMPORT PRESET above).
- **LOCKED by mistake:** an applied booster edit (for example Integrated Extinguishers) no longer shows as LOCKED, as if another mod had changed it.
- **Revert all / Reset to defaults:**
  - Values still on their way are no longer applied after a revert.
  - With only failed or still-applying fields left, it no longer says "nothing to reset".
  - A failed field is reset too.
  - Reset rows stay in Changes as **REMOVING** until the game's value is back.
- **Big applies:** a value waiting its turn behind many others (a 300-field preset) is no longer reported as failed while HD2Runtime still applies it. Starting a large apply no longer causes one long frame.

Tested offline against the HD2Runtime 0.31.2 source (and 0.31.1 during development). The payload part fix was confirmed in game. HD2Runtime 0.31.2 itself fixes the custom stratagem stutter and the "CONFLICT" on a second Beam Conversion edit: update HD2Runtime for those.

### Install
Install `HD2REditor-0.9.3.zip` with your mod manager (Echelon or HD2 Arsenal), next to HD2Runtime 0.31.2. It replaces earlier versions.

## Since 0.9.0

### New in 0.9.2
- **Missile direct hit:** the FAF-14 Spear, MLS-4X Commando and StA-X3 W.A.S.P. get "Damage · Missile direct hit": the damage of the missile itself (the Spear's 4000), separate from its explosion.
- **P-2 Peacemaker and P-19 Redeemer:** Projectile and Damage sections (their round lives on the ammunition).
- **AC-8 Autocannon flak:** "Projectile · Flak", "Damage · Flak", "Explosion · Flak" and a flak swap. The GR-8 Recoilless Rifle's HE round and the RL-77's cluster round get the same rows.
- **Ammunition swaps:** "Projectile · Swap" on the ammunition of the Liberator, Dominator, Diligence, Breaker, Peacemaker and Redeemer.
- **Statuses on grenades:** throwables get an "Explosion · Status" section. Change the statuses a grenade applies, or add one in its first free slot, for example a third status on the G-4 Gas.
- **Settings:** a button that opens HD2Runtime's own settings (F10): its language, interface size and the new live performance graph.

### Fixed in 0.9.2
- **SEARCH:** clicking a result opens exactly that result, whatever filter BROWSE was left on. The results are whole objects only (weapons, stratagems, boosters, grenades ...), not single fields.

### Fixed in 0.9.1: your saved values survive a bad start
- **No more re-applying after every launch.** With "Restore my last applied values" on, a value that could not be applied when the game started (for example, refused that time because of another mod, or a custom stratagem that was not registered yet) was erased from your saved values, so you had to apply it again by hand. It now stays saved and is tried again at the next start, until you change or reset that field yourself (or use Reset to defaults).
- **Changes are saved straight away.** One field still waiting to apply (for example a value whose game data only loads in a mission) no longer stops everything else you changed in that game from being saved.
- **A clearer log:** the startup line in HD2Runtime.log now names each saved value that did not come back, and why.

### New in 0.9.0: find and protect
- **SEARCH tab:** find anything the editor can edit, by name.
- **Values another mod owns are locked:** a value changed by a mod outside HD2Runtime (a data-file mod, another memory editor), or a component table another mod moved, is shown read-only with the reason instead of failing when you apply.
- **Translations in every game language:** Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Russian text draws with the game's own fonts (issue #8). The language picker counts only characters no game font has.

### Weapons (0.9.0)
- **Beams:** beam swaps (the LAS-5 Scythe's included), the beam fire mode by name (continuous / charged blast / pulsed), and the pulse duration, which limits a pulsed beam's fire rate. The rows explain how.
- **Beam Conversion** (HD2Runtime 0.31.0): a group on every projectile weapon that can fire a Trident-style beam instead. It shows the verdict and caveats, the switch, the fire rate (up to 900 rpm), beams per pulse, pulse length, and per-weapon damage, AP and range. Unequip the weapon first. Solo, or a lobby where everyone runs the same Runtime with the same conversions.
- **Wind-up:** a Wind-up section with the firing charge (set it to 0 for an instant first shot, for example the Sickle) and the spin-up of the Maxigun, the Patriot minigun and the Maelstrom gun. The derived warm-up and the cooldown after an overheat are shown read-only, with the fields to edit.
- **Fire modes:** a second or third mode on a single-mode weapon (for example the Hot-Shot) adds the game's fire-mode selector in the same change. Up to three modes.
- **Team-reload weapons** (GR-8, AC-8, RL-77, Spear, W.A.S.P.): their own spare magazines or rounds (used before the backpack, so they can reload without it) and every backpack's ammunition.
- **Missiles:** the flight of the W.A.S.P., Spear, Commando, P-33 and P-92 missiles.
- **Support weapons:** projectile swaps and the Breaching Hammer's blast.

### Vehicles, backpacks and stratagems (0.9.0)
- **Mech arms and mounted guns:** a projectile swap per mount, spread on every projectile mount, and status effects on vehicle and drone weapons.
- **EXO-55 Breakthrough:** the shield arm's health, and the shield plate's health and armor.
- **Hover Pack:** its flight values sit under "Flight". The launch value it never reads is locked. Raise the low-speed vertical acceleration above 9.82 to climb.
- **Charges before cooldown:** any stratagem can join the Eagle Rearm pool, so its uses become charges refilled by Eagle Rearm. It shares the Eagles' cooldown.
- **Orbital Railcannon:** search radius, tracking speed, beam duration and shot delay. The Orbital Laser gets a re-target interval.
- **Status Effects:** a new category for gas, fire and every other status effect's damage and duration.

### Fixed in 0.9.0
- Turret traverse limits use exact-value handles (no more TURRET_LIMIT_ORDER refusals).
- Joining the Eagle Rearm pool and changing mission uses in the same apply no longer depends on order.
- Sentry wind-up, heat, handling, ammo and beam rows sit under the right "Mounted Weapon" sections, and backpack ammunition sections have readable names.
